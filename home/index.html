Help

Questions and help

Phoenix Photo is in early access. Support is by our feedback form, and we reply within a few business days.

Do I need a Google account?

Yes. Phoenix Photo runs inside your own Google account and keeps everything in your Google Drive. A free Gmail account works.

Google says the app "isn't verified." Is it safe?

Yes. Phoenix Photo installs as your own private copy inside your Google account, so Google shows this warning for it the same way it does for any personal project. Our install guide shows each screen and which buttons to press. Your files stay in your Drive and are never sent to us.

I'm stuck during installation. What should I do?

Most problems come from skipping a step on Google's permission screens. Go back through the install guide one screen at a time, and make sure you choose "Select all" when Google lists the permissions. Still stuck? Send us a note through the feedback form with a screenshot, and we'll help.

Something stopped working partway through a big upload.

Free Google accounts have daily limits on how much work an app can do. If you add hundreds of photos at once, Phoenix Photo may pause and pick up again later. Your photos are not lost. Try smaller batches, or wait a few hours and continue.

Is there an app to download?

No. It runs in your web browser on a computer, tablet or phone. You can add it to your phone's home screen like an app.

Will editing change my original photos?

Never. Your original photos, documents and recordings are kept exactly as you added them. Every crop, color fix or restore is saved as a separate version that you can undo at any time.

Who can see my photos and stories?

Only you and the people you share with. Everything stays in your Drive, and we never have a copy.

Will my archive still be there in 20 years?

Yes. Your photos, documents and recordings are ordinary files in your Google Drive, so they stay yours even if you stop using Phoenix Photo.
