Privacy

Last updated November 2026

The short version: Phoenix Photo Archive runs inside your own Google account. Your photos, documents, recordings and stories are stored in your Google Drive, and we never receive a copy of them.

This website

This website does not use cookies, tracking or advertising. It does not ask for your name or email address. If you send us feedback through our feedback form, we only see what you choose to write in it.

The Phoenix Photo Archive app

When you install Phoenix Photo Archive, it is added to your own Google account and runs as you. Everything it saves, including photos, documents, videos, voice recordings, stories, labels and books, is kept as ordinary files in your Google Drive and in a spreadsheet in your Drive. You can see, move, download or delete those files at any time.

Google will ask you to approve a set of permissions when you install it. Phoenix Photo Archive uses them to:

  • Google Drive and Sheets: store and organize your photos, documents and recordings, and keep your archive's catalog.
  • Send email as you: send the invitations and share links that you choose to send to family or contributors.
  • Connect to outside services: work with Google's file services, and connect to an AI service only if you choose to turn that feature on (see below).
  • Your email address: show who is signed in and who added each photo or story.

Your original files

Phoenix Photo Archive never changes your original photos, documents or recordings. Every edit, such as a crop, a color fix or a restore, is saved as a separate copy, and your original stays exactly as you added it.

Sharing and contributors

Only the people you invite can see your archive or add photos and stories to it. Contributors need an account that you approve. You can remove access at any time.

Optional AI keyword suggestions

Phoenix Photo Archive can suggest keywords for your photos using Claude, an AI service from Anthropic. This is turned off unless you add your own Claude API key in Settings. When it is on, the photo you ask about and its existing labels are sent to Anthropic to generate suggestions. Anthropic's own privacy terms apply to that request.

If you stop using Phoenix Photo Archive

Your files stay in your Google Drive. Nothing is deleted when you stop using the app, and you never need us to get your memories back.

Questions

If you have a question about privacy, please reach us through the feedback form on our home page.
