Set up your own Phoenix Photo

Your photos, stories and recordings stay in your own Google account. Nobody else can see them unless you share them. New versions arrive by themselves.

A Google accountGmail or Google Workspace
About 15 minutesOnce. Updates are automatic after that.
A computerfor setup. Phones work fine after that.
Tip: start with a new Google account just for your archive. A free Google account comes with 15 GB of storage shared by Gmail, Drive and Photos. A separate account gives your archive all of that space (you can buy more later through Google One), keeps it apart from your personal email, and makes it easy to share with or hand down to another family member later. Create one at accounts.google.com/signup, then sign in with it before step 1.

  1. Make your own copy

    Open the Phoenix Photo template while you're signed in to Google. You land on its ⓘ Overview page. Click the copy icon at the top right (two overlapping pages; it says "Make a copy" when you point at it).

  2. Name it

    Your copy opens in the Editor, named Copy of Phoenix Photo. Click that name at the top and change it to Phoenix Photo. Don't change anything else.

  3. Publish it as a web app

    Click the blue Deploy ▾ button, then New deployment. On the left, under "Select type", you should see Web app. If you don't, click the gear and choose Web app. Check these two boxes:

    Execute as: Me (your email)
    Who has access: Anyone

    Then click Deploy. "Anyone" lets family and contributors open your pages without a Google account. Your archive still asks each person to sign in or use a passcode.

  4. Allow access

    Google asks first. Click through these screens in order:

    1. Authorize access, then pick your account.
    2. "Google hasn't verified this app": click Advanced, then Go to Phoenix Photo (unsafe).
    3. "Sign in to Phoenix Photo": click Continue.
    4. "Select what Phoenix Photo can access": tick ☑ Select all, then Continue.

    Tick Select all. Phoenix Photo needs every one of those boxes (your Drive for the photos, a spreadsheet for the details, email for invitations, and running at night for updates). If one is left unticked, setup won't work.
    Why "unverified"? This copy belongs to you and runs only in your account, so Google shows the warning for any script someone sets up for themselves. Phoenix Photo can only reach what's in your own Google account, and it never sends your photos anywhere.

    Using a work or school Google account? Your administrator may need to allow apps like this first.

  5. Copy your web address and finish setup

    You'll see "Deployment successfully updated". Under Web app, click ⧉ Copy under the URL (not the Deployment ID), then Done.

    Open a new browser tab, paste the address and press Enter. Type a name for your archive (for example, "The Smith family photos") and your name, then click Set up Phoenix Photo. It takes about a minute.

    Signed in to more than one Google account? Open the address in a private (Incognito) window and sign in with only the account that made the copy.

  6. Bookmark it and add it to your phone

    The last screen shows three links: the Library (for you and your helpers), the Family view, and the Submissions page with its passcode. Bookmark the Library. To put Phoenix Photo on your phone or desktop, open the Library and go to Settings › Links & app icon.

Keeping it up to date

Nothing to do. Every night your copy checks for a new version, makes sure it really comes from the publisher, and installs it. You get a short email when it updates. If something isn't right, Settings › Updates › Go back returns to the version before.

Once in a while a new version needs a new Code.gs. If so, Phoenix Photo emails you the steps, and everything keeps working on the current version until you do it.

Getting help

In the Library, open Settings › Updates › Send a problem report. It sends a short note with the version and any error messages, never your photos.

Your photos are yours

Everything lives in a Phoenix Photo folder in your Google Drive, as ordinary files. Settings › Storage & import › Save a full copy saves all the information into one Google Sheet. To keep a copy off Google, use Google Takeout.
