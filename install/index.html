Set up your own Phoenix Photo

Your photos, stories and recordings stay in your own Google account. Nobody else can see them unless you share them. New versions arrive by themselves.

A Google accountGmail or Google Workspace
About 15 minutesOnce. Updates are automatic after that.
A computerfor setup. Phones work fine after that.

  1. Make your own copy

    Open the Phoenix Photo template while you're signed in to Google. On the left, click ⓘ Overview, then ⧉ Make a copy at the top right.

    Template link coming soon

  2. Name it

    Your copy opens. Click its name at the top (it says Copy of Phoenix Photo) and change it to Phoenix Photo.

  3. Publish it as a web app

    Click the blue Deploy button, then New deployment. Click the gear next to "Select type" and choose Web app. Set:

    Execute as: Me
    Who has access: Anyone

    Then click Deploy. "Anyone" lets family and contributors open your pages without a Google account. Your archive still asks each person to sign in or use a passcode.

  4. Allow access

    Google asks you to allow Phoenix Photo to use your Drive, Sheets and email. Click Authorize access and choose your account.

    You'll then see "Google hasn't verified this app". Click Advanced, then Go to Phoenix Photo (unsafe), then Allow.

    Why the warning? This copy belongs to you and runs only in your account, so Google shows it for any script that someone sets up for themselves. Phoenix Photo can only reach what's in your own Google account, and it never sends your photos anywhere.

    Using a work or school Google account? Your administrator may need to allow apps like this first.

  5. Finish setup in your browser

    Google shows the Web app link. Copy it and open it in your browser. Type a name for your archive (for example, "The Smith family photos") and your name, then click Set up Phoenix Photo. It takes about a minute.

    Signed in to more than one Google account? Open the link in a private (Incognito) window and sign in with only the account that made the copy.

  6. Bookmark it and add it to your phone

    The last screen shows three links: the Library (for you and your helpers), the Family view, and the Submissions page with its passcode. Bookmark the Library. To put Phoenix Photo on your phone or desktop, open the Library and go to Settings › Links & app icon.

Keeping it up to date

Nothing to do. Every night your copy checks for a new version, makes sure it really comes from the publisher, and installs it. You get a short email when it updates. If something isn't right, Settings › Updates › Go back returns to the version before.

Once in a while a new version needs a new Code.gs. If so, Phoenix Photo emails you the steps, and everything keeps working on the current version until you do it.

Getting help

In the Library, open Settings › Updates › Send a problem report. It sends a short note with the version and any error messages, never your photos.

Your photos are yours

Everything lives in a Phoenix Photo folder in your Google Drive, as ordinary files. Settings › Storage & import › Save a full copy saves all the information into one Google Sheet. To keep a copy off Google, use Google Takeout.
